Getting started

  1. Try Load two-house example for a ready-made schedule. Use fictional data in the public demo.
  2. To start your own plan, choose New project, name it and create a blank project. When a workspace is open, Add project adds another without replacing it. Create from template is another starting point.
  3. In Resources & calendars, add people or equipment. New resources default to 40 hours per week: eight hours Monday through Friday. Adjust availability and exceptions to match your plan.
  4. In Tasks, choose Add task. Enter the primary resource, good and poor estimates in days, and any predecessor task IDs in Depends on. Supporting resources share the full task interval; the primary resource controls the effort estimate.
  5. Choose Recalculate schedule after edits, then inspect forecasts and the timeline. Save your workspace to retain changes.

Higher projects get first choice of shared resources among eligible tasks. Dependencies, active work and locked commitments still apply.

Forecasts & deadlines

P50 is the model's middle completion estimate. P95 is a more conservative target; P99 is further into the estimated late tail. These forecasts use independent task durations and a fixed critical chain. They are approximate, not guarantees.

Task inputs are good days · P20 and poor days · P80, rather than minimum and maximum durations. Bars show the deterministic schedule, not P95-duration tasks.

The P95 target status compares the project's P95 completion with its deadline. A November 11 forecast is late for a November 2 deadline, and on time for a November 11 or later deadline. Calendar deadlines include the named date. A deadline warns about risk; it does not force tasks to finish by that date.

With a calendar, Time display switches between dates and day offsets without recalculation. Without a calendar, results use abstract days. Estimates remain in days; clock-time appointments are not supported.

Forecast withheld means held or blocked work prevents a full completion forecast. It does not by itself mean the schedule is infeasible.

Timeline

  1. Choose Highlight project for the project whose critical chain you want to inspect. Amber bars belong to that chain, including controlling work in other projects.
  2. Choose Show links → All dependencies and waits to see every recorded connection. The default Critical chain & feeders filters other links. Solid blue lines are dependencies; dashed purple lines are resource waits.
  3. Click a task to inspect its details. Find task jumps to a row; predecessor buttons navigate to related tasks. Scroll to see both ends of cross-project connections.

Scrolling to another project or selecting its task does not change Highlight project. Green bars do not mean that project has no critical chain.

If P2-T002 depends on P1-T001, highlight Project Two and show all links to inspect that connection. Applied feeding protection appears as a hatched band after the feeder's task bar. Select it for the calculated P99 − P50 protection; it is estimated protection, not a guarantee.

Use Zoom for detail. Pending edits do not move the bars until recalculation succeeds.

Compare

  1. Calculate your current workspace, open Compare and choose Set current as baseline. Download the baseline if you want to keep it after leaving.
  2. Change priorities, estimates, resources or tasks. Recalculate the scenario.
  3. Choose Compare versions. Inspect project P95 shifts, changed tasks, resources and conflicts. Earlier or later alone does not establish which scenario is best.

You can also choose a saved workspace file as the baseline without replacing the current workspace. Deleted tasks appear as Removed; held work can appear as suspended. Comments and checklist ticks alone do not mark a schedule changed.

Both plans are recalculated with the current engine. Comparisons require the same workspace identity, progress date and calendar origin. Recalculate pending edits first. The captured baseline is kept in memory; opening another workspace or refreshing clears it. Save workspace does not include that captured baseline.

Save & reopen

  1. Edit Workspace name to give the plan a useful name.
  2. Recalculate changes, then choose Save workspace. Keep the downloaded JSON file in a folder you can find. Empty or unassigned plans can also be saved as drafts.
  3. Choose Open saved workspace and select that file to continue. Keep separate files for your original plan and promising scenarios.

There is no automatic cloud saving or account history. On the hosted demo, calculations run on the server, but workspaces are not saved there. Other visitors have separate browser state. Download before refreshing, closing the page or loading another workspace.

Checklist ticks record steps, not task completion. Change task status and remaining estimates in Progress when recording actual work.

Common problems

Tasks need resources
Add resources in Resources & calendars, then assign every primary and supporting requirement. Explicitly remove a supporting requirement you no longer need. Unassigned tasks block scheduling.
Delete task is blocked
Another task still depends on it, possibly in another project or on hold. The error names the dependents. Remove or reassign those dependencies before retrying. Deletion requires confirmation and keeps the resource list. Save a copy first if you may need to restore the task.
No visible critical chain or connections
Check Highlight project and Show links in Timeline. Scroll to see cross-project endpoints. Empty projects have no chain; held or blocked work can withhold full forecasts. Recalculate pending edits and check errors above the workspace.
A project is on hold
New unlocked work is suspended; active work continues and locked commitments stay reserved. Dependent work can be blocked too. Resume the project and recalculate to restore its eligible work.
Feeding protection was not applied
Read the explanation under Calculated feeding protection in Feeding tasks. The scheduler retains forward timing if protection cannot fit safely. Equal duration estimates can produce a zero-length buffer.
Results look out of date
Check the calculation status and errors, then recalculate. After a software update, refresh the browser. Save your workspace first.
